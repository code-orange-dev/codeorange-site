The program

Three parts, one pipeline.

Workshops bring developers in, the Privacy Track teaches them, and fellowships support the best of them to keep going.

01

Privacy Track

24 bi-weekly sessions, open-source curriculum, drop-in at any point.

  • Season One rewritten to open with Silent Payments (BIP352)
  • New labs: Silent Payments sender and scanner, chain analysis, compact block filters, payjoin, network privacy
  • Launch announced for 6 October; Code Review Club for 8 October
  • Curriculum (CC0)
02

Fellowships

Paid fellowships for the people who teach and build.

  • Cohort 1: three educator fellows running programs
  • Clear criteria for developer fellows written and published
  • First developer fellow joined in October
  • Fellowship guidelines
03

Workshops

The entry point: free sessions online and in person.

  • Nine sessions across Discord, Bali and Cebu
  • Topics from mining and self-custody to AI agents paying in sats
  • Every session on Luma with automatic reminders
  • Calendar
