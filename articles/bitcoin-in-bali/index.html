Bitcoin in Bali: Where to Learn in Person.

Free workshops, meetups and builder sessions at Bitcoin House Bali in Canggu, and how to join.

By AndyNovember 8, 20264 min read

Bali is full of people who hold bitcoin. Few of them have ever met another bitcoiner face to face, set up a hardware wallet with someone who could answer their questions, or watched a node sync on a real machine. If you are searching for Bitcoin in Bali, this guide shows you where to go, what happens there and how to join, for free.

We are Code Orange Dev School, and we run our in-person sessions at Bitcoin House Bali in Canggu every month.

Why learn Bitcoin in person?

You can read about Bitcoin online forever. But some things are much easier with people around you:

  • Self-custody. Writing down your first seed phrase, or checking an address on a hardware wallet screen, feels scary alone. With someone next to you, it takes ten minutes.
  • Hardware. You can hold a signing device, a node and even a miner before you spend money on one.
  • Questions. "Is this normal?" is the most common question we hear. In a room, you get the answer right away.
  • Friends. Bitcoin is a long game. People who learn with others keep going.

We wrote more about this in Just Show Up.

Bitcoin House Bali: our home base

All our in-person sessions happen at Bitcoin House Bali, Jl. Kayu Manis, Canggu (Google Maps). It is a space for bitcoiners, so you will usually meet a few more on the way in.

Sessions start at 5 PM Bali time (WITA, UTC+8), which fits nicely after a surf or a work day.

Bitcoin meetups in Bali: what happens each month

Bitcoin Workshop (every 4th Friday)

Our main hands-on meetup. Each month we pick one or two topics and work on them together:

  • Self-custody: setting up and testing a wallet, backups, and multi-vendor multisig.
  • Full nodes: what a node does, and how to run your own.
  • Mining: how it works, with real hardware on the table.
  • Privacy: Lightning, eCash mints and tools like PayJoin.

Newcomers are welcome, and so are cypherpunks. You leave with better Bitcoin knowledge or a new bitcoiner friend, usually both.

Agentic Engineering and Vibe Coding (every 4th Tuesday)

A hands-on meetup for using AI tools to build things faster: websites, small apps, scripts and agents that can pay in sats over Lightning. You do not need to be a programmer. Bring a laptop and an idea.

Free Bitcoin sessions online, all month

Between meetups, the community keeps going on Discord:

  • Privacy Track: 1st and 3rd Tuesday, hands-on Bitcoin privacy from PayJoin to Silent Payments.
  • Bitcoin Reading Club: 4th Wednesday, *The Bitcoin Standard* chapter by chapter.
  • Code Review Club: we read real Bitcoin pull requests together.
  • Decoding Bitcoin: a free 10-week study cohort starting 16 November 2026.

Every date is on our calendar, which updates automatically.

Who comes to our Bali Bitcoin workshops?

A typical evening has digital nomads, local Indonesian builders, long-time holders who finally want to self-custody, and developers who want to contribute to Bitcoin open source. Some people come once to fix one thing. Others come every month and end up teaching.

If you want to go further, our developer fellowship supports builders working on Bitcoin and Nostr projects.

Can you pay with bitcoin in Bali?

People often ask this. In Indonesia the rupiah is the only legal currency for payments, and Bank Indonesia does not allow crypto as a payment method. So most shops will not take bitcoin at the till, even in Canggu. Bitcoin in Bali is mostly about saving, learning and building, not spending. Before you try, check current rules and BTC Map for any places that list themselves.

What to bring

  • A charged phone with space for one wallet app.
  • A laptop if you want to code or run a node (optional).
  • Your hardware wallet if you already have one and want help with it.
  • Never bring or share your seed phrase. Nobody at a real Bitcoin meetup will ever ask for it.

How to join

It is free and you do not need to register for the in-person meetups. Just show up at 5 PM.

The easiest first step: join the Code Orange Discord, check the calendar for the next session, and come say hi. New to Bitcoin? Start with our Start here page.

Frequently asked questions

Are there Bitcoin meetups in Bali?

Yes. Code Orange Dev School runs free Bitcoin workshops at Bitcoin House Bali in Canggu every 4th Friday, and an AI and vibe coding meetup every 4th Tuesday, both at 5 PM WITA.

Where is Bitcoin House Bali?

On Jl. Kayu Manis in Canggu, North Kuta, Badung. See the map.

Do I need to know about Bitcoin to come?

No. Beginners are welcome, and we explain everything from the start.

Are the workshops free?

Yes. All Code Orange sessions, in person and online, are free.

Can I learn Bitcoin development in Bali?

Yes. Come to the in-person workshops, then join our online programs such as Decoding Bitcoin, the Privacy Track and Code Review Club. See our guide How to Become a Bitcoin Developer.
