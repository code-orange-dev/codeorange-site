Curriculum

One chapter a week.

Read the chapter before Monday. Questions are shared out evenly, so each week some of them are yours. Answer them in the cohort channel on Discord and tick them off here. Chapters link to the free online edition of the book.

How it works

No accounts. Just show up.

Sign up once with your name and Discord username. You get a personal link that lets you tick things off from any device.

Read the chapter

Each week has one chapter of the free online book, sometimes with an optional extra chapter for keen readers.

Answer your questions

Every question has one owner. Look for your name, post your answer in the cohort channel on Discord before Monday, and tick it done.

Do the exercises

Everyone does the same hands-on exercises with Bitcoin Core on signet or regtest. Share what you got and tick each one off.

Meet on Monday

We talk it all through on Discord, Mondays at 7pm UTC+8. Week 10 is graduation and your next step into open source.

The cohort

Who's in, and how it's going.

Students are listed in sign-up order. Questions count the ones assigned to each person; exercises count across all ten weeks.

Sign up

Join the cohort.

Free of charge for everyone joining. All you need is a Discord account and a few hours a week.

Your name and progress show on this page. Your Discord username and email are only seen by the organisers.

The book

Read it free, online.
