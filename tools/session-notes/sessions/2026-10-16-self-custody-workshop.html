Highlights

Can you sleep well at night? The call opened with a simple question. If your whole stack sits on one Ledger, Trezor or Coldcard and you read about a supply chain attack on that brand, what then? The call brought up recent news of a Ledger supply chain attack through resellers and a Coldcard exploit. Spreading your keys across different vendors means one bad device can’t take your coins.

1Build the 2-of-3 in Sparrow

Andy built the wallet live in Sparrow with three hardware wallets from three different companies, then spent from it with two of them.

  • New wallet → policy type Multi Signature, slider set to 2 of 3.
  • Keys 1 and 2: plug in, enter the PIN, Connected Hardware Wallet → Scan → Import Keystore.
  • Key 3 (air-gapped): on the Coldcard, Advanced Tools → Export Wallet → Sparrow, then load the JSON file from the microSD card.
  • Spend: Trezor signs over USB, then the unsigned transaction (a PSBT) goes to the Coldcard on the microSD card, gets signed and comes back to Sparrow to broadcast.

Check: each key’s derivation path should read m/48'/0'/0'/2', the standard path for native SegWit multisig (BIP 48).

2Restore it in another app

What if Sparrow stops being maintained one day? Andy rebuilt the same wallet in Nunchuk: export the wallet from Sparrow (Settings → Export), then in Nunchuk choose Recover existing wallet → via BSMS/descriptor. He added the three keys again, signed with the Ledger and the Trezor, and sent the rest of the coins. Importing the raw descriptor needed manual edits, so the exported file was the easier route.

Key takeaways

1. The wallet file matters as much as the keys. The output descriptor is the blueprint of a multisig. With only two seeds and no descriptor, rebuilding the wallet is very hard. Anyone who finds it can see your balance and history, but can’t spend.

Seed phraseCan spend (with one other key). Keep each one in a different place. Output descriptorCan’t spend, but reveals every transaction. Keep copies in many places. Hardware walletHolds one key. Buy it straight from the maker, never a reseller.
